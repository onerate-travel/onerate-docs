Everything after sign-in lives under **/panel**. What you see depends on your role — an agent's
portal has fewer screens than an owner's. See [Roles and permissions](/en/start/roles/).

## The navigation

The menu is split into two groups.

**Operations** — the daily work:

| Screen | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Run a search across your suppliers and book from the results. |
| **Bookings** | Every booking your agency has made. |
| **Customer Bookings** | Bookings made by your customers' own users, if you use customers. |
| **Reports** | Read-only totals and a CSV export of what you sold. |

**Management** — setting the agency up:

| Screen | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Suppliers** | Connect, test and enable the suppliers you hold contracts with. |
| **Customers** | Your own corporate customers and the people you invite into them. |
| **Preferences** | Rules that override cheapest-first ranking. |
| **Team** | Colleagues, their roles, and pending invitations. |
| **Settings** | Agency identity, contact details, configuration, map, subscription. |

**My account** is separate from both: it is your own name, password, two-factor setup and active
sessions, and every role has one.

## The header bar

The bar across the top of every panel screen carries the three things that change what you see
everywhere at once.

### Default margin

Your agency's margin on every supplier rate, as a percentage. Change it here, press **Apply**, and
every price the portal shows from then on includes it.

If it is unset, nothing is added — you are selling at the supplier's net price. The field says so.
See [Pricing and margin](/en/manage/pricing/).

### Currency and language

- **Language** — a drop-down of the seven languages the portal speaks (English, Türkçe,
  Български, Magyar, Italiano, Polski, Română), remembered on this browser. This is a display
  choice and nothing else.
- **Currency** — this is **not** a display choice. It is the currency your agency *sells in*, and
  changing it changes which offers are ranked in your main results list. Nothing is converted. Only
  an owner or admin can change it; everyone else sees it read-only.

### Your account

Your name, a link to [My account](/en/account/security/), and **Sign out**.

### Agency switcher

If you belong to more than one agency — or you are a platform user with access to several — a
switcher appears here. It changes whose data you are looking at. Check it before you book.

## Notifications

A tray in the corner carries short-lived messages: a booking confirmed, a setting saved, an action
that failed. Each can be dismissed. They are announced to screen readers as they arrive.

Separately, the navigation shows a count when bookings **need your attention** — bookings sitting
in a status that will not resolve itself. Follow it to the list; see
[Booking statuses](/en/booking/statuses/).

## Things that are true on every screen

- **The language switcher is always present.** No screen is English-only, and every language is
  reachable from every screen.
- **Amounts are formatted `tr-TR`** — `1.250,00`, not `1,250.00` — and use aligned figures so a
  column of prices reads straight down.
- **A page you cannot reach says so.** If your role does not permit a screen, it is not in your
  menu; opening its address directly gives you a page telling you so, not a blank screen.
- **A mistyped address gives a real "page not found"** with a way back, in your language.
- **Leaving a screen with unsaved changes asks first.** You are offered **Discard changes** or
  **Keep editing**.

## Two habits worth forming

1. **Check the agency in the switcher before you book.** The portal will happily book against
   whichever agency is selected.
2. **Read the supplier status line on a search before you tell a customer "there is nothing
   available".** "No hotels found" and "no hotels found, and two suppliers did not answer" are
   different sentences. The portal distinguishes them; make sure you do too.
