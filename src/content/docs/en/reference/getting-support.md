## Where it is

**Support** is in the portal's left-hand navigation, at the foot. It is there for every role — if
you can sign in, you can write to OneRate.

`support.onerate.travel` goes to the same place. It is a shortcut worth saving; it will ask you to
sign in first, because a support ticket is tied to your agency.

## Before you write

[Troubleshooting](/en/reference/troubleshooting/) lists the messages the portal shows and what each
one means. Most questions are answered there in less time than a ticket takes, and the ones that are
not are the ones OneRate most wants to see.

:::caution
**A booking in *Reconciling* is urgent.** Its outcome is unknown, not failed. Open a ticket, and do
not book the same stay again — see [Booking statuses](/en/booking/statuses/#reconciling).
:::

## Opening a ticket

Pick what it is **About**, give it a **Subject**, and describe **What happened**.

| About | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| A booking | Anything about a specific stay — a wrong voucher, a status that will not move, a cancellation fee you did not expect. |
| Billing | Your subscription and invoices. |
| The portal | A screen that will not load, a form that refuses valid input, a search that answers nothing. |
| Something else | Anything that is none of the above. |

What makes a ticket answerable quickly:

- **The booking reference**, if there is one. It is on the booking's own page.
- **What you expected instead.** "The voucher says room only" and "the voucher should say half board,
  because the confirmation does" are different tickets, and only the second can be checked.
- **When it happened**, if it is not happening now.

Two questions about the same booking are two tickets. Opening a second one does not overwrite the
first.

## Who can see it

| Your role | What you see under Support |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, Administrator | Every ticket anyone at your agency has opened |
| Agent | The tickets you opened yourself |

This is not a setting and it cannot be changed per ticket. If you want a colleague to follow a
ticket you opened, an owner or an administrator can already read it.

**Customers do not use this screen.** A corporate client with portal access talks to *your* agency,
not to OneRate — their questions come to you.

## What happens next

OneRate replies **in the portal**, in the same thread. There is no email notification yet, so check
back under Support; a ticket with a new reply sorts to the top of the list.

You can keep replying to a ticket for as long as it is open.

## Closing

OneRate closes a ticket when it is answered. A closed ticket stays readable, and its thread is the
record of what was decided — but it takes no more replies. If the question is still live, open a new
one and say what has changed.
